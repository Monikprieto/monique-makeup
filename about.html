About Me

Technique, creativity, and precision.

A certified hairstylist and professional makeup artist.

My work focuses on creating looks that highlight each person's individuality, whether through structured hairstyles, editorial makeup, or elegant transformations for special events, photography, and television. I believe beauty is about refining and enhancing your best features.

With training in hair structure, color harmony, facial analysis, and modern styling techniques, I approach each project with professionalism, attention to detail, and an artistic vision.

I am committed to embracing industry trends and delivering timeless and impactful results.
